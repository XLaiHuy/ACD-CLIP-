# PCRR method review

PCRR is a peer-relative witness-rank transform. It receives only common
query-peer cosine `c`, peer Gram upper triangles `G`, and validity. It never
sees files, GT, model outputs beyond the frozen geometry, or another family.
The two witness pools, two witness aggregations, and two stage aggregations
are the complete preregistered 8-config space. No distance temperature,
threshold, K, radius, or outcome-dependent choice exists.
