support the harm concern and harm-aware safety component.  P13 supplies a
strictly post-hoc, GT-bearing indication of latent patch-level opportunity but
does not establish a deployable selector.  P20 shows weak image-level value
prediction.  P23 validly completed one 12-class attempt with audit parity
`1.1102230246251565e-16`; A0 and A1 were safe and improved all classes, yet
both failed the preregistered pAP H3 headroom requirement.

closed.  More image-level budgets, an image-level value Ridge/RankNet, and an
alpha sweep are not justified by the current evidence.  The branch that
remains scientifically justified is a **new**, explicitly preregistered
patch-level actionability study.  Its GT-free benefit predictability remains
unknown, so it must not be described as an established final architecture.

MVTec and Medical remain untouched; P24 made no CLIP forward, model fit, or
scientific attempt.  Next allowed action: explicit user review only.
