# P24 Oracle Comparability

P13 D4 (`0.5995204096824335` pAP) and P23 A1
(`0.5714452773668350` pAP) answer different questions and are not
contradictory.

P13 D4 is a **POST_HOC_ORACLE_DIAGNOSTIC**: a GT-bearing patch-level cohort of
rejected, sign-correct actions.  It demonstrates latent opportunity under that
specific counterfactual.  It is neither a deployable policy nor a global
oracle, and it is not an upper bound on a future learned controller.

P23 A1 is **PREREGISTERED_SCIENCE** for a coarse image-level coordinate witness
whose permitted actions are NATIVE, SAFE20, SAFE30, and EXPAND40.  It measures
the headroom of that frozen image-level family, including its own baseline,
image scope, action constraints, and coordinate interactions.

The two records differ in intervention granularity, allowed actions, baseline,
scope, GT use, and interaction structure.  P23 therefore does not falsify
patch-level opportunity.  It rejects the much narrower claim that this coarse
image-level action family contains enough pAP headroom for the frozen final
contextual-controller criterion.
