# P25R2 Numerical Contract

Batch=1 frozen deployment is the numerical reference.  The selected production
route batches candidates of one image up to the already-frozen panel cap of 16.
Every candidate row replicates the same frozen native logits, applies one
source-sign correction, then calls the unmodified frozen industrial deployment
function.  Candidate score maps must never be constructed by sparse
post-deployment basis addition.

The pre-prereg candle fixture comprises the first deterministic 128 panel rows.
For batch=1 versus production batching require the recorded numerical envelope,
no stable score-order disagreement, no benefit-sign disagreement outside
`BENEFIT_EPS`, and direct exact-metrics versus grouped replacement AP parity.
The frozen envelope is zero on this fixture and `BENEFIT_EPS=1e-10`.
