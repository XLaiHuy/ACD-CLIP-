# P17 Resume Contract

`ATTEMPT_STARTED.json` stores UUID, execution SHA, prereg SHA, and input
hashes. A mechanical parent restart may process only missing worker folds when
all identities and compact checkpoint hashes match, completed folds remain
immutable, no code/protocol changed, and `RESUME_LOG.jsonl` is appended. Any
mismatch refuses resume. P16 artifacts are engineering evidence only.
